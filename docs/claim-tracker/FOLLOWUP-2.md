# Community claim tracker: independent adversarial follow-up review

Reviewed October 14, 2026. Target: `codex/claim-tracker-v1` at **b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3**, draft [PR #6](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/pull/6).

## Verdict

**Ready after specified revisions.** The core manual workflow is coherent enough to preserve; it does not need redesign. The latest edits implement the concrete historical N1–N3 and F11/F13 requests. However, specificity is not an abuse-resistance test, emergency hiding still permits indefinite concealment, and the enabled merge/undo surface lacks settled reconciliation rules. These are remaining design risks, not reproduced implementation failures.

The three most consequential changes are:

1. Bound repeated specific-but-unsubstantiated correction reports and manual evidence suggestions without blocking urgent privacy or reviewer-initiated correction work.
2. Give emergency hides an accountable, time-bounded disposition process and a visible safe history of the hide; preserve the current fail-safe against automatic republication.
3. Explicitly disable unsafe assessed-claim merge/undo cases in the pilot, or specify atomic reconciliation and dependent-evidence checks before enabling them.

This is an independent challenge of the supplied written response, performed with AI assistance. It is not independent expert certification. I read the root AGENTS.md, v1.md, review.md, review-detailed.md, FOLLOWUP.md, REVISIONS.md, and claude.md at the target commit. GitHub reported PR #6 open and draft, with no recorded reviews or comments at inspection. No application was run, no test below was executed, no legal or platform permission was certified, and no tracker was deployed or published. Historical verdicts remain attached to their historical commits.

## Remaining findings and minimal changes

Severity reflects plausible consequence in a 10–20 member private pilot. All sequences below are hypothetical. No critical finding is established.

### D1 — Specificity and an open-report cap do not bound adversarial throughput

**Medium; design risk / missing requirement; before manual launch.** Sources: [v1.md:58](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L58), [v1.md:130](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L130), [v1.md:136](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L136); A10/A12. Historical N1/R1/R13/R20/R21.

The rule explicitly checks “specificity, not truth.” A member names evidence E and asserts a precise but invented change. Once triaged, the banner appears. On dismissal the slot opens, so the member immediately submits another specific allegation; other invitees can do likewise. Members can also repeatedly add detail to an existing report. Coalescing the same change does not coalesce invented different changes. The cap bounds concurrent report count (up to 40 across 20 members), not triage arrivals or reviewer work. Priority slots can be consumed indefinitely, and a specificity banner can repeatedly cast doubt even though it accurately describes its limited meaning.

**Minimal change:** Add configurable per-member rolling admission and report-edit limits, task-level deduplication, and an operator abuse disposition that suppresses repeat disproven reports absent genuinely new evidence. Preserve a private reporting route and urgent privacy escalation; never let a new-intake pause block reviewer-initiated decisive retractions. Distinguish receipt, specificity, and corroborated-change states. Keep the qualified specificity banner if desired, but prohibit reactivating it for a previously dismissed same allegation without a reviewer-recorded new basis. A global workload alert/admission policy must not silently discard urgent corrections.

**T1:** Submit two vague reports concurrently and a third: no banner; third rejected without payload retention. Submit a specific fabricated change: only specificity notice after triage, never an assessment change; dismiss with reason. Resubmit the same disproven change with paraphrases and repeated edits: no fresh task/banner without new basis. Across multiple accounts exceed configured rolling limits: attempts remain bounded under the stated policy; operator alerted; a reviewer-confirmed sole-source retraction still enters priority reassessment and produces Unresolved. Test cap enforcement atomically under concurrent requests.

This extends N1; it does not deny that the exact historical two-vague-plus-third scenario is now specified correctly.

### D2 — Emergency hide can conceal prior wording indefinitely

**Medium; design risk / missing requirement; settle before implementing hide disposition.** Sources: [v1.md:34](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L34), [v1.md:140–142](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L140-L142), A8. R8/R22.

A reviewer invokes a suspected listed basis on an embarrassing revision. The record is hidden; disposition exceeds 24 hours. The spec deliberately says material “stays hidden” and alerts the operator. The operator may be the same person and can leave it unresolved indefinitely. Labels/dates/handles survive, so this is not audit erasure, but members lose the explanatory wording the history was intended to preserve. Closed reason enums do not establish that a basis is valid.

**Minimal change:** Require affected spans, safe suspected-basis rationale, named disposition owner and decision deadline, a persistent visible safe hide event, escalation to the project owner or another designated accountable role if overdue, and a recorded decision to uphold redaction or restore safe content. For an author’s own assessment, require a separately identified escalation recipient even if no second reviewer exists. Keep unsafe payload hidden while overdue; do not auto-restore on a timer. Display unresolved availability status/age without disclosing sensitive text. The pilot may use a manual owner review; no elaborate moderation system is needed.

**T2:** Reviewer hides their own revision under a listed suspected basis; safe labels/dates/handle and hide event remain visible. At 24 hours unresolved, unsafe text remains inaccessible across pages/inbox/search, and escalation is delivered to the named recipient. Reject “I disagree with my old assessment” even if wrapped in a listed category without a specific basis. An adjudicated unsupported hide restores only safe content with an audit event; upheld redaction replaces only restricted spans and records same-author action. Retry disposition without duplicate events.

N2's enumerated-basis and same-person disclosure requirements are resolved; this finding is a remaining emergency-path risk.

### D3 — Deferred merge reconciliation needs an explicit disabled boundary

**Medium; missing requirement / specification ambiguity; before enabling assessed merges or unsafe undo.** Sources: [v1.md:128](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L128), [REVISIONS.md:39](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/REVISIONS.md#L39), original [review-detailed.md:110–117](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/review-detailed.md#L110-L117). R2/R14, A4/A8/A9.

v1 permits reviewer merges, transfers follows and supports undo. Two equivalent standing claims have conflicting assessments. Materially different assertions are protected, but equivalent assertions with conflicting dated assessments are not reconciled by any required destination revision. After merge, a later destination assessment cites evidence associated with the merged claim; undo returns that evidence and can leave a misleading destination assessment. REVISIONS explicitly acknowledges F10 and says unsafe operations must remain unavailable, but v1/A4 do not enumerate which operations are disabled or test that boundary. Do not report F10 as a newly discovered absent control: the deferral is documented, but operationally ambiguous.

**Minimal change (recommended pilot scope):** Put the safety boundary in v1: block merging two already-assessed records until reconciliation rules exist; allow private duplicate attachment to an assessed record without transferring a verdict. Block undo if any later revision depends on reassigned evidence, unless it atomically creates the required reassessment state and explicit association/follow decisions. Alternatively implement the original F10 destination revision and undo-invalidation rules.

**T3:** Attempt Supported/Contradicted assessed merge: rejected with no changes if deferred. Safe private attachment reveals no private wording/counts and transfers no verdict. Merge then add dependent evidence/revision; unsafe undo rejected atomically, or a permitted undo flags affected assessments for review in the same commit. Histories remain reachable; active followers receive exactly one required notice; follow choices never silently duplicate.

### D4 — Reviewer sightings can bypass the substance of attribution opt-in

**Medium; design risk; clarify before manual launch.** Sources: [v1.md:44](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L44), [v1.md:157](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L157), A11. R6/R18 and original F9.

A member submits a link from a tiny forum and declines attribution. A reviewer opens that very link, then records it as their “own public sighting.” Nothing requires independent discovery or a separate disclosure review. The public link/community can identify the contributor just as member-supplied attribution would. Public visibility of the post does not establish that publishing its connection to this pilot is harmless.

**Minimal change:** Reviewer viewing of member-provided context cannot by itself convert it to independent attribution or defeat the member's default-off preference. A separately discovered sighting needs documented provenance and a privacy check; omit community/link where it would expose private contributor context. Keep a safe as-circulating paraphrase and factual assessment without the attribution when feasible.

**T4:** With opt-in off, reviewer opens the submitted small-forum URL and attempts reviewer attribution: blocked or omitted from pages/filters/inbox. Genuine independent public discovery follows the documented privacy rule rather than automatically bypassing it. With explicit member opt-in, display attribution as member-supplied and limited; verify no member identity is exposed.

### D5 — New-claim caps do not bound manual evidence or recurring review work

**Medium; design risk / missing workload requirement; before manual launch.** Sources: [v1.md:21](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L21), [v1.md:56–60](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L56-L60), [v1.md:112](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L112), [v1.md:116](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L116), [v1.md:144–146](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L144-L146); A7/A10/A12. R1/R9/R13/R20.

Members may propose evidence without the automatic-stage suggestion caps applying. A member submits hundreds of low-value links to an existing claim without opening correction reports. Meanwhile each published assessment defaults to a seven-day review date; published records accumulate even when open new intake is capped at 20. Two daily reserved slots and an open-candidate ceiling do not bound evidence triage or the recurring workload. The spec honestly calls throughput proposed, but it has no admission/monitoring budget for these paths. This can crowd out meaningful corrections without violating the new-submission cap.

**Minimal change:** Bound pending manual evidence suggestions per member/claim and request size, coalesce duplicates, and meter edits. Track total review minutes and active monitoring workload, not merely count of claims. Define when new intake pauses because scheduled checks/corrections exceed capacity and when an operator may archive monitoring with a reason and follower notice. Do not expire admitted urgent corrections as a workload shortcut. Configure limits before launch and revise from measured pilot work; this is not a promise that any arbitrary numeric default is sufficient.

**T5:** At manual-suggestion limits, concurrent extra suggestions are rejected without attempted-payload retention; duplicate links do not create independent-source credit. Advance a fixture of many published claims to their review dates: capacity warning and intake pause activate under the defined workload rule; each completed check records dated results, overdue claims remain visibly overdue, and a decisive correction retains priority. Archive monitoring only with explicit reason/history and one follower notice. Measure human time during the pilot; fixture success alone cannot validate throughput.

### D6 — Export and retention clauses need explicit scope and deadline semantics

**Low; specification ambiguity / acceptance precision; settle during data-model design.** Sources: [v1.md:60](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L60), [v1.md:98](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L98), [v1.md:144–146](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L144-L146), [v1.md:157](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L157), [v1.md:175](https://github.com/ExtiVaries/How-to-Hear-a-Bully/blob/b6356d5e87226936fe1c83abffa3843a275758c3/docs/claim-tracker/v1.md#L175); A11/R19/R23.

“Private context never enters … exports” is unqualified, while operator-created operational exports are allowed and subjected to private-payload retention. This can mean either exports exclude all private context or only member-visible exports do. A11's “Active copies/logs contain no payload” also needs to mean the purged payload after its deadline, not every legitimate unexpired private submission. These are wording ambiguities, not demonstrated leaks. Working-day deadlines also need one calendar/timezone definition to make boundary checks reproducible.

**Minimal change:** State explicitly that private context never enters member-visible exports; if operational exports may contain it, enumerate restricted copies and enforce purge/restore rules on them. Alternatively forbid private context in all exports and say operational exports are content-free. Define pilot timezone/working days and absolute acceptance/expiry/deletion timestamps. Expired or terminal unpublished records cannot be published or reopened to evade deletion; a new submission follows ordinary intake instead. Clarify any legal hold is separately authorized, scoped, reviewable and restricted, rather than an undocumented deadline reset.

**T6:** With a fake clock at deadline-plus-grace, race publication, expiry, archive, merge and reopen: one consistent terminal/publication outcome under the explicit boundary rule; no clock reset. At deletion deadline, original pointers/URLs/text, drafts, summaries, logs and allowed operational exports are absent. Restore a pre-deletion backup: deletion manifest applies before any access. Verify the 30-day backup expiry separately from seven-day active-copy purge. An authorized narrowly scoped preservation hold is restricted and recorded; ordinary reviewers cannot invent one. Unexpired own submissions remain readable as A1 requires.
