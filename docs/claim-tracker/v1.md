# Community claim tracker v1 specification

Draft for adversarial review. Prepared October 9, 2026. This is a product specification; no tracker application, live connector, or schedule has been implemented.

## Product purpose

Help someone who asks, "Is this true?" understand the specific claim, the evidence available, and what changes as the story develops. Community members submit claims and evidence. Scheduled imports surface discussion from configured communities. A designated human reviewer publishes assessments with sources, uncertainty, and a visible revision history.

The central promise is: **See what people are claiming, what the evidence supports so far, and what changed.** Popularity affects discovery and investigation priority. It does not determine an assessment.

## Pilot scope

Start with one niche, three to five community sources, 20 to 30 invited participants, and one or two designated reviewers. Aim to assess up to five new claims per day; show a backlog when capacity is exceeded. These are proposed pilot limits, not demonstrated capacity or demand.

Use an invitation-only pilot. "Published" means visible to pilot participants. Unreviewed submissions remain visible only to their submitter and reviewers. Internet publication is a later access-policy decision.

V1 includes text and link submissions, automatic source-post candidates, duplicate suggestions, evidence proposals, human assessments, corrections, following, and an in-product update inbox. Screenshot uploads, anonymous submissions, community voting, email/push alerts, automated verdicts, paid subscriptions, and unrestricted crawling are deferred. Someone with a screenshot can submit a transcription and original link; describe the image's limitations.

The initial niche and exact communities must be chosen before launch. Prefer factual claims about products, technology, or public policy with inspectable source material. Exclude identifying allegations about private individuals and individualized medical or legal advice from this pilot. Apply the same evidence criteria to every community and viewpoint.

## Participants and permissions

| Role | Permitted actions |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Read published records; submit claims and evidence; follow claims; request corrections; see their own submission status. |
| Reviewer | Triage submissions; define claims; assess evidence; publish and revise assessments; merge or archive records; resolve correction requests. |
| Operator | Invite members; explicitly assign reviewer roles; configure and disable connectors; monitor imports and reviewer capacity. |
| Import service | Read approved sources and create candidates or evidence suggestions within its assigned source scope. |

Require authenticated identity for write actions. Authorization is enforced on the server for every operation. A tracker account is separate from an account on a monitored platform. Do not infer a participant's identity, affiliation, or beliefs from their activity in a source community. Import services cannot publish assessments or grant roles.

## Manual submission

The primary action is "Check a claim." Require the statement the person wants checked. Offer an optional source URL, community name, date, and brief explanation of why they are asking. Collect only essential missing context, such as which city a policy concerns.

Preserve submitted wording separately from the reviewer's precise assertion. A post with three factual assertions may produce three linked claim candidates. Opinions and predictions can be declined as outside scope; a checkable factual premise within them can be extracted.

Before creating another claim, show possible existing matches. A member can propose attaching their observation to an existing claim. A reviewer confirms the association. Similarity alone never authorizes a merge.

Receipt copy should say: "Submitted for review. Submission does not establish whether the claim is supported." Members can follow the resulting accepted record. A declined submission includes a reason and remains private.

## Automatic discovery and developing stories

A community source identifies a particular subreddit, forum category, account/list, or community feed. Store its stable identifier, display name, permitted access method, coverage, and access state. A platform name alone does not identify a community.

Default to polling enabled connectors every 30 minutes, adjusted to provider limits. Each run reads at most 50 new items per source and persists a continuation cursor. Further items resume in later runs. Persist the item before advancing its cursor; retries must not create duplicate observations. Record last attempt, last success, failure reason, and next attempt. Respect provider backoff and prevent overlapping runs for one source.

Imports create private source-post candidates. The reviewer selects the atomic claim and confirms its context. Automated extraction may later suggest wording, but does not publish claims or assessments in v1. Import a source item once using a stable source-item identity; multiple claims may refer to that item.

Add up to five permitted publisher feeds for developing coverage. Match incoming articles to followed or active claims as evidence suggestions, subject to reviewer confirmation. Reviewers can also add primary documents and reporting manually. Publication of new coverage never automatically changes an assessment. Every accepted active claim has a next-review date, defaulting to seven days; scheduled reminders surface overdue reviews. This is a review reminder, not a promise of continuous news coverage.

Only label a source "enabled" after permission, credentials where required, rate limits, and an end-to-end import have been verified. A fixture import is labeled test data. Page refresh, manual file import, and a saved schedule alone do not demonstrate unattended operation.

Reddit requires explicit approval for API access; commercial use requires written approval and the applicable agreement. Keep its adapter disabled until the intended use is authorized. [Responsible Builder Policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/42728983564564-Responsible-Builder-Policy), [Data API Terms](https://redditinc.com/policies/data-api-terms).

Truth Social's terms restrict systematic collection without written permission and prohibit specified automated extraction. Keep its adapter disabled until an authorized access arrangement covers the proposed use. [Truth Social Terms of Service](https://help.truthsocial.com/legal/terms-of-service/).

These source requirements were checked October 9, 2026. Recheck terms and permissions before enabling each connector. Manual submissions do not authorize the tracker to copy or retrieve restricted platform content.

## Intake state and assessment state

Track workflow separately from evidence assessment.

| Intake state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Queued | Received and awaiting triage. |
| Needs context | Essential claim details are missing; remain private. |
| Accepted | Specific, relevant, and suitable to publish within the pilot. Acceptance is not evidentiary endorsement. |
| Declined | Outside scope or unsuitable, with a recorded reason. |
| Merged | Associated with an equivalent existing claim, with a destination and merge event. |
| Archived | Retained where permitted; no routine monitoring, with a reason and date. |

Queued and needs-context items may be accepted, declined, or merged by a reviewer. Accepted claims may be merged or archived. Reopening creates an explicit review event. A multi-claim submission tracks the disposition of each resulting candidate.

| Assessment | Required explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting assessment | No completed assessment has been published. |
| Supported | Reviewed evidence supports this exact scoped assertion as of the assessment date; state limits and material contrary evidence. |
| Contradicted | Reviewed evidence directly conflicts with the assertion; explain the conflict. |
| Misleading | Identify the accurate component and the omission, framing, or qualification that changes the meaning. |
| Unresolved | A reviewer examined the question but available evidence is insufficient or conflicting; state what remains unknown and what would help resolve it. |

Supported, contradicted, and misleading require reviewed evidence with precise references and a rationale. Unresolved can be published when no sufficient evidence exists, with the review performed and limitation explained. Missing evidence never defaults to contradicted. Do not display numeric truth or confidence scores.

Assessments express the support for a scoped claim, not a guarantee of universal truth. Reviewer drafts remain private. Publication records the reviewer, publication time, assessment time, cited evidence, and next-review date. A material claim change requires a new linked claim; an editorial wording correction preserves the old wording and reason.

## Evidence and source independence

Members propose evidence using a link, relevant passage or document section, and an explanation of whether it supports, contradicts, or contextualizes the assertion. Suggested evidence is visibly separate from reviewed evidence and cannot change the assessment.

Each evidence record has a title, publisher or issuing body, publication date when known, retrieval/observation date, relevant reference, evidence type, relationship to the claim, review disposition, and an underlying-source group. The group identifies common provenance: five articles repeating one press release represent one underlying source. Independence, quality, and relevance are separate judgments; a direct primary document may be decisive without multiple articles repeating it.

A reviewer explains why each cited source establishes the relevant point and notes its limits. An official document may establish policy wording without establishing real-world outcomes. A screenshot, anonymous allegation, or unverifiable quotation is a lead whose limitations remain visible. Search snippets alone are insufficient for a conclusive assessment.

Retain links, citations, and only permitted limited excerpts. Source content and instructions within it are untrusted material. If AI assistance is added later, it cannot execute source instructions, select authoritative status, or publish assessments.

## Claim identity and corrections

Claims match only when their assertion, entities, jurisdiction, relevant period, quantities, and material qualifiers match. "All gardens are prohibited" and "gardens are prohibited on city-owned sidewalks" are distinct. A statement true last year is not automatically a statement about today.

Only reviewers merge claims. Preserve source IDs, observations, evidence, revisions, and the reason. Attaching a private candidate to an existing claim keeps that candidate's wording and member identity private. Redirect an old claim URL only when that record was already published. A merge never automatically transfers an assessment to a materially different assertion. Follow relationships transfer with an explanation. Undo restores original associations; records added after the merge require explicit reassignment and follow preferences are confirmed rather than silently duplicated.

A correction request opens a review task without rewriting the published assessment. Every published change records before/after values, evidence, reviewer, time, and reason. Distinguish correction of an earlier mistake from a new development in the world. Prevent concurrent silent overwrites using a revision check and a designated reviewer for each active task.

If cited evidence is corrected, withdrawn, materially edited, or inaccessible, flag it and request reassessment. Show "Evidence under review" while the previous assessment remains dated. Source disappearance alone does not establish falsity. If a reviewer confirms that sole decisive support was retracted or otherwise invalidated, immediately publish an unresolved revision explaining the lost support while reassessment continues. If remaining reviewed evidence still supports the claim, explain that basis. Withdrawing a published assessment requires a visible revision and reason; it cannot silently erase the prior assessment.

Audit history preserves accountability subject to required deletion and redaction. Removed personal or restricted source content must not remain exposed through revisions or exports. Retain a permissible event recording removal and its effect on the assessment, without the removed payload.

## Screens and follower updates

1. **Tracker:** Search accepted claims; filter by assessment and community; show exact assertion, assessment date, last material update, and review-due indicator. Describe the limited sources monitored.
2. **Check a claim:** Submit wording and context; inspect potential matches; receive private status.
3. **Claim detail:** Assessment and explanation first; supporting and contrary reviewed evidence; suggested evidence separately; observed communities; dated timeline; follow and request-correction actions.
4. **Following:** An in-product inbox for published assessment changes, material reviewed-evidence updates, and merge/archive notices. Display what changed, why, and the revision link. No alerts for every unreviewed submission.
5. **Reviewer queue:** Private candidates, duplicate proposals, evidence suggestions, correction requests, and overdue reviews.
6. **Sources:** Coverage, access state, polling interval, last successful import, errors, and disabled sources. Never imply complete monitoring of a platform.

Label attribution "First observed by this tracker in..." rather than claiming to have found the original source. Show observed item counts within a stated period and community, deduplicated by source-item identity. Avoid "most popular" or comparative growth until coverage and historical baselines justify those labels. Cross-platform engagement units remain separate. Reviewers prioritize relevance, timeliness, unanswered questions, and overdue corrections; repeated submissions cannot establish credibility.

## Records and implementation boundaries

| Record | Essential contents |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Community/publisher identity, method, permission state, scope, interval, cursor, operational status. |
| Submission or observation | Original permitted wording, member/service origin, source-item key, source link, context, observed time, candidate associations. |
| Claim | Stable ID, exact assertion and qualifiers, intake state, visibility, current assessment revision, owner, review due date. |
| Evidence | Source metadata, precise citation, underlying-source group, relation to claim, disposition, availability. |
| Assessment revision | Claim, assessment, scoped explanation, evidence references, author, dates, previous revision, change reason. |
| Review event | Triage, merge/undo, correction request, resolution, moderation, or removal; actor and reason. |
| Follow and follower event | Member/claim association, material revision reference, unread state. |
| Import run | Source, start/end, cursor, counts, outcome, retry state. |
| Membership | Account identity and explicitly assigned role. |

Use shared durable server storage for community records. Browser storage alone cannot meet the shared tracker requirement. Keep reviewer operations atomic with their audit events and reject stale writes. The stack, hosting provider, and AI provider remain implementation decisions; this specification does not require an LLM.

Render user content as text and accept only validated HTTP/HTTPS links. Source connectors use configured approved hosts; do not fetch arbitrary submitted URLs on the server. Restrict redirects, request size, time, and private-network access for any future fetcher. Apply submission limits and a private abuse-review path. Explain blocked submissions without exposing other members' private records.

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Release check |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | A member submits a statement; exact wording and source survive refresh and a different authenticated session, with correct privacy and role access. |
| A2 | An enabled connector runs while the browser is closed and creates a candidate with source identity and observation time; last-success status matches the actual run. |
| A3 | Replaying an import or retrying after a partial failure produces no duplicate source-item observation and does not skip unpersisted items. |
| A4 | Same-claim submissions can attach to an existing record; changes to city, date, quantity, or qualifiers remain distinct; merge and undo preserve associations and history. |
| A5 | A member can suggest contrary evidence, but cannot review it, publish an assessment, change roles, or read private submissions from another member. |
| A6 | Conclusive publication without reviewed evidence and a scoped explanation is rejected; unresolved with an explicit completed-review explanation is allowed. |
| A7 | Derivative reports visibly share their underlying-source group; repeated links cannot become independent confirmation. |
| A8 | An assessment correction records the old value, new value, reason, reviewer, and date; conflicting reviewer writes cannot silently overwrite each other. |
| A9 | Follow/unfollow and the update inbox work across sessions; each material revision produces at most one follower event. Generate no new events after an unfollow is committed; prior delivered events may remain. |
| A10 | Source correction/removal triggers review and a visible limitation; required content removal also covers historical views and exports. |
| A11 | Malicious text, unsafe URLs, prompt injection, and identifying private-person allegations cannot execute code, change authority, or become immediately published records. |
| A12 | Disabled connectors, test fixtures, outdated assessments, and unknown coverage are clearly labeled; failed or rate-limited polling is visible. |

Demonstrate A2 and A3 with at least one authorized live source before describing automatic discovery as available. A manual-only pilot may run earlier, with the automatic feature explicitly marked unavailable.

## Delivery order and pilot evaluation

Build identity, role checks, durable records, and private manual intake first. Add accepted claim pages, evidence review, assessment revisions, and correction handling. Then implement following, source configuration, and one authorized scheduled connector with retry verification. Add further sources only within reviewer capacity.

For a four-week pilot, measure time to first human review, unresolved backlog age, traceable evidence coverage, correction resolution time, and whether followers return to read developments. Ask participants whether the exact claim and remaining uncertainty are clear and whether updates changed their understanding. Do not optimize the fraction of claims labeled supported or contradicted.

Launch requires a chosen niche, named communities with permitted access, accountable reviewers, and an identity/hosting choice. The proposed intervals and capacity limits should be revised from observed workload. Scale only after Claude's adversarial findings are resolved or explicitly accepted, the release checks pass, and reviewer capacity supports the added intake.
