# What would help right now?

Find the official route. Understand a claim. Decide what deserves action.

These are free resources with sources you can inspect, clear limits, and practical next steps. Start with whichever question is yours; there is no set order.

### Don't Pay a Middleman

Before paying for help with a U.S. federal service, check the official route and its fees. Sometimes paid help is worth it. See what a paid helper may add.

**[Find an official service](https://dont-pay-a-middleman.vercel.app/)**

### How to Hear a Bully

Learn to examine manipulative uses of language and tell them apart from disagreement, mistakes and honest corrections. Then apply the same questions to speakers you support.

**[Read the Bully guide](../../how-to-hear-a-bully.md)**

### Why Does Everything Feel Like a Crisis?

Explore what is happening, what reaches you, whom it affects, and what action fits. Sometimes the answer is more urgency, not less.

**[Read the Crisis guide](../crisis-guide/why-does-everything-feel-like-a-crisis.md)**
