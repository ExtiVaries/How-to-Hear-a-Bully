## Work completed

- Targeted checks of original research and official guidance. The complete handoff records each source's inspection scope and access limitations. No underlying study data were reanalyzed.
- Separate source-research and adversarial passes, coordinated through AI agents. This is not human peer review, reader testing, or a systematic literature review.
- A five-question teaching method, two explicitly fictional exercises, and an ordered drafting prompt. The method is not presented as a validated instrument.
- Adversarial assessment of authority, blanket skepticism, false balance, corrections, conflicts, access burdens, and the title's scope.

Two findings from that assessment changed the brief: it now distinguishes changed circumstances, revised inference, and a mistaken earlier report without assuming an error was preventable; and it tells the drafter to use most guardrails to test wording rather than insert a separate caveat for each one.

The fictional market arithmetic is consistent: 100 to 200 is a doubling; 300 to 200 is a decrease of one third. The exercise states comparable counting rules and does not infer a causal effect from the two totals. The library scenario places the reader after the stated reopening date.

The handoff deliberately omits media-brand rankings, current political cases, extra historical anecdotes, detailed medical guidance, and unrelated changes to existing guides. The source packet is not a new live guide or an endorsement by any cited institution.
